# Program-table leaves are the fact table; project lists are separate drill-downs

The canonical fact table is the GAB program-table leaves (rows with nothing printed beneath them), because they tie to every
declared total in 628/632 agency-copies. DPWH Vol I-C projects and the FMR/NIA/HFEP/SBDP annexes are separate drill-down
tables linked to a parent leaf with a coverage ratio, never unioned into the fact table. We rejected "project rows as leaves +
a synthetic undisclosed-remainder row": it gives finer rows but invents lines the bill never printed, and DPWH projects only
cover ~99.6% of their parent and ~₱320–355B of them have no parsed DEO.
